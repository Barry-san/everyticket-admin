# Everyticket Administration

Vocabulary for administering the Everyticket platform across organizers and events.

## Language

**Administrator**:
A member of Everyticket's platform administration team who manages activity across the app. Organizers use a separate dashboard.
_Avoid_: Organizer (when referring to the operator of this app)

**Organizer**:
A person or organization that runs events on Everyticket and manages them through a separate organizer dashboard.
_Avoid_: Administrator

**Event**:
An experience published by an organizer and offered through Everyticket. Administrators may feature, suspend, or restore it.

**Order**:
A customer's ticket booking for an event, retaining its payment attempts and the fee agreed at checkout.
_Avoid_: Payment (when referring to the booking)

**Payment exception**:
A verified payment requiring an explicit decision before ticket fulfilment, such as a late payment or an amount mismatch.

**Refund**:
A tracked request to return specified ticket and platform-fee amounts to a customer. Approval starts processing; gateway confirmation establishes completion.
_Avoid_: Completed refund (when only approval has occurred)

**Payout**:
Money owed to an organizer for an event, excluding platform fees. Its transfer is made externally and recorded with a reference.
_Avoid_: Platform revenue

**Fee rule**:
A versioned fixed or percentage platform charge applied to future orders from its effective time. Existing orders retain their original fee.
